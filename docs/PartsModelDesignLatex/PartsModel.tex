\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
A rocket in QtRocket is a tree of physical components, and nearly everything the program does ---
editing a design in the CLI, painting the GUI tree view, rendering the 3D visualizer, integrating a
flight --- is a walk over that tree. This whitepaper documents the architecture that carries all of
those roles: a strict three-way decomposition into \textbf{\code{Part}}, a pure leaf that knows one
component's mass, geometry profile, and aerodynamic contribution and nothing else;
\textbf{\code{PartNode}}, the owned tree node that binds a leaf to its placement link, its children,
and the cached composite quantities of its sub-tree; and \textbf{\code{PartsModel}}, the single
mutation authority that owns the root, indexes every node by stable id, enforces the single-motor
invariant, and narrates every edit through a typed aboutTo/did event stream.

The load-bearing commitments are few and strict. Ownership is linear (\code{unique\_ptr}
end-to-end), so attaching a part \emph{is} surrendering it: double-attachment, ownership cycles, and
retained mutable aliases are unrepresentable rather than checked. Reads hand out \code{const}
references; the only post-attach writes are routed verbs on \code{PartsModel} that perform the edit
and then invalidate exactly the caches the edit dirtied. Composite mass, CM, and inertia rebuild
behind a mass-delta gate keyed on a live mass sum --- cheap enough to run every integrator stage,
exact enough to freeze bit-for-bit after burnout --- while geometry resolves once per structural
edit and never during a burn. Structural errors are typed values, a self-intersecting design is a
hard located failure on every read, and ``no design'' is a representable state, not a placeholder.

The document specifies each class contract with UML (PlantUML) and the control flow with activity
and component diagrams (Ti\emph{k}Z), walks the two execution paths end to end --- a CLI design
session from \code{newdesign} to \code{savedesign}, and a simulated flight from ignition through
the burn to the post-burnout cache freeze --- and records the design decisions and their rejected
alternatives with the rationale that selected them.

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{designrule}[Three non-negotiable rules]
\textbf{1. One authority mutates.} Every post-attach edit is a \code{PartsModel} verb that writes
and then dirties the correct cache chain; reads are \code{const}. \textbf{2. Ownership is linear.}
Nodes own leaves and children uniquely; the type system, not a runtime check, forbids aliasing.
\textbf{3. Derived state is gated, never trusted.} Composite quantities rebuild when their inputs
moved and freeze when they did not --- and a failed geometry solve throws on \emph{every} read.
\end{designrule}
