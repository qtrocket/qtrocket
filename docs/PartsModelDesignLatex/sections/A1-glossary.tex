\section{Glossary}\label{sec:glossary}

This appendix defines the terms of art as they are used throughout this whitepaper, in
alphabetical order. Every entry names a construct in the working tree and cites where it
lives; the definitions are deliberately terse --- the load-bearing discussion is in the
body sections cross-referenced from each entry.

\begin{description}

\item[aboutTo/did event pair] One mutation's change notification, fired twice: once with
  \code{before == true} immediately before the write lands, once with \code{before ==
  false} immediately after (\code{PartsModel::Event},
  \src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{144}). The pairing exists because Qt's
  \code{QAbstractItemModel} row-op contract demands \code{begin*} before the model
  mutates and \code{end*} after; the stream is shaped so a Qt adapter is a pure
  translation (\cref{sec:gui}).

\item[Composite (mass / CM / inertia)] The aggregate of a node together with all its
  descendants: total mass, mass-weighted center of mass, and the full
  $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ inertia tensor about that CM, computed by the two-pass walk
  \code{PartNode::computeCompositeAt} (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{171}) and served
  by \code{compositeMass}/\code{compositeCm}/\code{compositeI}. Any node is a valid
  sub-assembly root for these queries (\cref{sec:tree}).

\item[Detached sub-tree] A \cppinline|std::unique_ptr<PartNode>| owned by no
  \code{PartsModel}: the return value of \code{detach}, the product of
  \code{PartNode::make}/\code{addChild}/\code{clone}, and the currency of serializer
  load and clipboard-style workflows. It is a first-class value --- it re-enters a model
  only through \code{attachSubtree} or \code{installRoot}
  (\srcf{core/model/PartsModel.h}).

\item[Gap (seat-signed)] The \code{gap} field of a \code{StationLink}: metres, with
  meaning selected by the seat --- a forward standoff along \zfwd\ for \seat{Abut} and
  \seat{OnSurface}, a non-negative insertion depth driving the child aft for
  \seat{NestInBore}. \code{placeChild} applies it as the signed term in
  $z_{\mathrm{child}} = z_p + \mathrm{signedGap} - z_c$
  (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{162}).

\item[Install facade] The single path by which a whole design enters or leaves the model:
  \code{RocketModel::installDesign} (\src{core/model/RocketModel.h}{87}) delegating to
  \code{PartsModel::installRoot} (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{220}). It atomically
  replaces the root (null clears), re-resolves the motor borrow, and applies the one
  install side effect owned by \code{RocketModel}: a fresh airframe does not inherit a
  manual reference-area override (\src{core/model/RocketModel.cpp}{121}).

\item[Leaf] What a \code{Part} is: one component's mass, geometry-derived inertia, CM
  offset, silhouette profile, and aero contribution --- and nothing else. Tree
  structure, placement, and every composite quantity live outside the leaf, in
  \code{PartNode}/\code{PartsModel} (\cref{sec:leaf}).

\item[Live sum] The uncached recursive mass total
  \code{PartNode::compositeMass(t)} (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{102}): this node's
  \code{getMass(t)} plus every descendant's, recomputed on each call. It is both the ODE
  mass divisor and the key the mass-delta gate compares against its stamp; it does no
  tensor work, which is why it is cheap enough to evaluate every integrator stage.

\item[Mass-delta gate] The cache gate for composite CM and inertia
  (\code{PartNode::ensureCompositeCache}, \src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{114}): rebuild
  when the structure was edited (\cppinline|massDirty_|) or when the live sum differs
  from the stamped \cppinline|builtAtMass_| under exact \code{==} comparison. The NaN
  sentinel forces the first build; after burnout the live sum repeats bit-for-bit and
  the cache freezes (\cref{sec:caching}).

\item[Motor borrow] The typed non-owning pointer \cppinline|part::Motor* motor_|
  (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{261}) aiming into the owned tree, giving the motor
  verbs $O(1)$ access to the one motor node. It is re-resolved only inside
  \code{registerSubtree}/\code{unregisterSubtree}, the adopt/release seam every
  structural verb funnels through, so no structural edit can leave it dangling
  (\cref{sec:motor}).

\item[\code{MutateHint}] The declared invalidation scope of an arbitrary leaf edit
  through \code{mutatePart} (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{139}): \code{MassOnly}
  dirties the mass chain alone, \code{Geometry} dirties both chains. The hint exists so
  a mass-only edit never pays for a geometry re-resolve and envelope sweep.

\item[No-design state] The representable empty model: \cppinline|root_| is null,
  \code{hasDesign()} is false, \code{size()} is 0, and every verb fails cleanly rather
  than operating on a placeholder rocket (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{161}). ``No
  design'' is a real state of the system, not an error (\cref{sec:tree}).

\item[\code{Part}] The pure-leaf base class of all rocket components
  (\srcf{core/model/parts/Part.h}): mass, per-unit-mass inertia, CM offset,
  length/radius profile, and Barrowman aero contribution, duplicated only via
  \code{clone()} (type-preserving, fresh id). Its mass/inertia setters are private to
  the \code{PartsModel} friend seam (\src{core/model/parts/Part.h}{133}), so
  post-attach mutation cannot bypass cache invalidation.

\item[\code{Part::Id}] The stable per-instance identity: a \cppinline|std::uint64_t|
  assigned at construction (fresh even for a copy), never changed, never serialized ---
  ids are meaningful only within one process run
  (\src{core/model/parts/Part.h}{114}). Names are human-facing labels and need not be
  unique; identity is id-only. \code{part::PartId} (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{25})
  is its dependency-free mirror in the placement layer.

\item[\code{PartNode}] One node of the owned tree (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{42}):
  it uniquely owns a \code{Part} leaf, stores the incoming \code{StationLink}, owns its
  child nodes, and carries the two gated caches (composite mass properties; resolved
  placements plus verdict). Reads hand out \code{const} references only.

\item[\code{PartsModel}] The single owner of the node tree and the only mutation
  authority (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{133}), owned by \code{RocketModel}. It holds
  the nullable root, the $O(1)$ id index, the motor borrow, and the change callback;
  every post-attach edit is one of its verbs.

\item[Per-unit-mass tensor] The geometric inertia tensor a leaf stores and
  \code{getI()} returns: units $\unit{m^2}$, taken about the part's own CM, independent
  of mass (\src{core/model/parts/Part.h}{59}). The composite pass multiplies it by
  \code{getMass(t)} and parallel-axis-shifts it, producing the full
  $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ tensor. Storing geometry, not mass-weighted values, keeps a
  time-varying mass (a burning motor) from invalidating the stored tensor.

\item[Placement gate] The structural cache gate
  (\code{PartNode::ensurePlacementCache}, \src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{144}):
  geometry is re-resolved and re-swept only when \cppinline|placementDirty_| is set ---
  a node attached or detached, a link or geometry edit --- and never on a mass change,
  because geometry is invariant under a burn (\cref{sec:caching}).

\item[Pose] A rigid placement in the root frame: a fore-plane origin on the axis plus an
  orientation quaternion, identity in 3-DOF
  (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{79}). \code{Pose::compose} is the rigid-transform
  composition law, degenerating to vector addition today and carrying 6-DOF at zero
  schema cost.

\item[Resolved placement (\code{Placed})] A part paired with its resolved \code{Pose}
  and its seat parent's id (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{98}), produced by the
  depth-first resolve \code{resolveNodes} (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{29}) and
  cached per node. The one resolve every consumer shares: composite pass, sweep, CLI
  reporting, visualizer (\cref{sec:placement}).

\item[Routed verb] A post-attach mutation path on \code{PartsModel} --- the structural
  verbs \code{attach}, \code{attachSubtree}, \code{detach}, \code{installRoot}; the leaf
  edits \code{setPartMass}, \code{setPartInertia}, \code{setLink}, \code{mutatePart};
  and the motor verbs: each performs the write, dirties exactly the cache chains the edit
  invalidated, and fires the paired events. Routing is what makes skipped invalidation
  unrepresentable rather than merely discouraged (\cref{sec:philosophy}).

\item[Seat kind] The closed three-value enum \code{SeatKind}
  (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{32}) naming the radial relationship of one
  attachment --- \seat{Abut}, \seat{NestInBore}, \seat{OnSurface}. The seat selects both
  the radial seam check and the sign convention of the gap. It is closed by design: a
  rigid axisymmetric stack has only these three radial relationships.

\item[Single-writer contract] The concurrency rule for every mutable-behind-const cache
  in the model layer: one thread reads and writes; concurrent access --- even all-const
  --- is a data race, because \code{const} accessors write cache fields
  (\src{core/model/PartsModel.h}{39}). The motor's log-once burnout latch is under the
  same contract (\src{core/model/MotorModel.h}{342}).

\item[Solid-host rule] The capacity predicate \code{Part::innerCapacityAt}
  (\src{core/model/parts/Part.h}{106}): the radius a host grants an intruder is its bore
  for a bored part and its outer skin for a solid one; an offender of outer radius $r$
  fits iff $r \le \mathrm{capacity} + \mathit{tol}$. One predicate, correct for both
  host kinds, consumed by the envelope sweep.

\item[Stamp-after-success] The failure discipline of the mass-delta gate: the stamp
  (\cppinline|builtAtMass_|, cleared \cppinline|massDirty_|) is written only after
  \code{computeCompositeAt} returns. A failed placement solve therefore throws out of
  the build and again on every subsequent read --- the cache never launders a failure
  into stale-but-plausible numbers (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{127}).

\item[Static margin / datum] \code{compositeAero} re-expresses each part's
  \cppinline|x_cp| (reported from that part's own CM) onto the sub-tree-root fore-plane
  datum --- the same datum \code{compositeCm} uses --- so
  $\mathrm{cp} - \mathrm{cg}$ is directly the static margin
  (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{224}). One shared datum is the point; two datums
  would make the margin a bookkeeping exercise.

\item[Station (fractional)] A position along a part's axis as a fraction
  $\code{station01} \in [0,1]$: $0$ the aft plane, $1$ the fore plane, mapped to
  $z = (\code{station01}-1)\,L$ in the part's \zfwd\ frame and resolved against the live
  \code{getLength()} on every read --- editing a length moves the seam
  (\src{core/model/parts/Part.h}{102}). \code{stationAt} returns the resolved
  \code{Station} landmark $\{z, r_{\mathrm{outer}}, r_{\mathrm{inner}}\}$, a derived
  value never stored.

\item[\code{StationLink}] The stored placement intent of one parent$\to$child edge
  (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{54}): parent fractional station, child fractional
  station, seat-signed gap, \code{SeatKind}, and a per-seam rotation (identity in
  3-DOF). A default link means ``child fore plane abuts parent aft plane,'' so the
  natural nose$\to$body stack costs no authored numbers. It is the only spatial
  relationship persisted; poses are always derived.

\item[Sweep (envelope sweep)] The whole-tree interference diagnostic
  \code{sweepOverlaps} (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{178}): build a world axial
  interval per part, sort ($O(N \log N)$), sample each offender at feature breakpoints,
  and test its outer radius against the smallest-id covering host's capacity under the
  solid-host rule, excluding the offender's descendants and direct seat parent. It runs
  once per structural resolve, never per integration step (\cref{sec:placement}).

\item[Verdict] The cached \code{SolveResult} of one structural resolve
  (\src{core/model/parts/Placement.h}{123}): an \code{ok} flag plus located
  \code{OverlapDiagnostic}s naming offender and host by id. \code{ok == false} makes
  every composite read throw (\src{core/model/PartsModel.cpp}{184}) --- a
  self-intersecting design is a hard, located failure, never a silently wrong mass or
  tensor.

\end{description}
