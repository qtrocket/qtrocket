\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
QtRocket currently links each child rocket component to its parent by a single vector: the child's
\emph{center of mass relative to the parent's center of mass} (CM-to-CM). Because a part's CM is, in
general, \emph{not} at its mid-length --- a solid cone's CM sits a quarter of the length from its base ---
this couples placement to mass distribution, forces authors to hand-compute every offset, and makes the
simulator and the 3D visualizer disagree by exactly the part's (geometric-center $-$ CM) offset. The
divergence has already produced a silently interpenetrating coupler in a saved design.

This whitepaper specifies the replacement: an \textbf{edge-defined, geometry-driven placement model}. Parent
and child are related by \emph{physical intent} --- a fractional axial station on each part, an axial gap,
and a seat kind (abut, nest-in-bore, on-surface) --- never an opaque offset. A \textbf{single resolver}
turns that intent into absolute geometry, and \emph{both} the simulator's composite-inertia pass and the
visualizer's mesh walk consume the identical result. The center of mass becomes a purely \emph{derived}
quantity. Extent-based diagnostics (a radial seam check, an $O(N\log N)$ envelope sweep with a correct
solid-host rule, and a degrees-of-freedom detector) turn the old silent interpenetration into a hard,
located failure. A path to 6-DOF is shaped into the data model at zero schema cost but deliberately left
unused.

The design is the adjudicated synthesis of five independent sketches, hardened against an adversarial
critique. This document renders that synthesis as a buildable specification: UML class diagrams (PlantUML),
activity and component diagrams (Ti\emph{k}Z), worked numerics against a real test fixture, and citations
pinned to the current working tree.

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{designrule}[Three non-negotiable rules]
\textbf{1. Placement is \emph{derived}}, recomputed by one resolver --- never store absolute coordinates or
the resolved pose. \textbf{2. The stored link is physical \emph{intent}} (feature-to-feature), never an
opaque offset; CM falls out as a consequence. \textbf{3. The common case costs \emph{zero} authored
numbers} --- a child with no link abuts its parent.
\end{designrule}
